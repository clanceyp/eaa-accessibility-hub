# Terms & Conditions

## Purpose of this site

The EAA Accessibility Hub is a free reference and news resource about
EN 301 549 and the European Accessibility Act (EAA). It is provided for
general informational purposes for web developers, testers, and anyone
working on digital accessibility compliance.

## Not legal advice

Content on this site — including the EN 301 549 overviews, the timeline,
and news entries about legal or regulatory action — is provided for
general information only. It is **not legal advice**, and should not be
relied on as a substitute for advice from a qualified professional or the
official text of the relevant standards and legislation.

Always check the authoritative source (the Official Journal of the
European Union, national legislation, or the official ETSI standard text)
before making compliance decisions.

## Accuracy

We aim to keep news and timeline entries accurate and up to date, and
every entry links to its original source. Errors can occur; if you spot
one, please let us know so it can be corrected.

## External links

enforcement bodies, and standards organisations. We are not responsible
