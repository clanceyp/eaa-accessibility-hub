# EN 301 549 — Web

EN 301 549 is the European standard for accessibility requirements suitable
for public procurement of ICT products and services. It is the technical
standard referenced by the **Web Accessibility Directive** (EU 2016/2102)
and the **European Accessibility Act** (EAA, Directive (EU) 2019/882) for
web content.

## How it relates to WCAG

For websites and web applications, **Clause 9** of EN 301 549 incorporates
the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) by reference. In practice,
meeting EN 301 549's web requirements means meeting **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**
(later versions of the standard move toward WCAG 2.2 AA).

That means the familiar four WCAG principles apply:

- **Perceivable** — text alternatives, captions, adaptable content,
  distinguishable contrast and presentation.
- **Operable** — keyboard accessibility, enough time, no seizure-inducing
  content, navigable structure, input modalities.
- **Understandable** — readable text, predictable behaviour, input
  assistance and error handling.
- **Robust** — compatible with current and future assistive technologies.

## What's in scope

- Public sector websites and intranets/extranets (Web Accessibility
  Directive)
- E-commerce, banking, transport ticketing/check-in, e-books, and other
  private-sector websites and web apps in scope of the EAA
- Web content delivered as part of a product or service, including
  authoring tools and content management systems used to produce it

## Other relevant clauses

EN 301 549 also includes clauses that commonly apply alongside Clause 9
for web-delivered services:

- **Clause 5** — Generic requirements (e.g. closed functionality)
- **Clause 6** — ICT with two-way voice communication
- **Clause 7** — ICT with video capabilities
- **Clause 11** — Software (relevant where a “web app” behaves like
  software, e.g. single-page applications)
- **Clause 12** — Documentation and support services
