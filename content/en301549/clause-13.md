# Clause 13 — ICT providing relay or emergency service access

Clause 13 sits alongside Clause 6 (two-way voice communication) and
focuses specifically on the public-interest communications layer:
relay services and emergency services.

## What it covers

- **Relay services** — where ICT supports two-way communication, it
  must not block a user's access to relay services, which let people
  using different communication modes (speech, text, sign language via
  video relay, speech-to-speech assistance) communicate with each
  other, typically via a human operator converting between modes.
- **Emergency services** — similarly, ICT supporting two-way
  communication must not block access to emergency services (such as
  112 in the EU), including support for real-time text to emergency
  services where technically feasible.

## Who this applies to

Products and services that support two-way communication and could
plausibly be a user's means of reaching a relay or emergency service —
phone systems, communication apps, and similar.
