# Clause 6 — ICT with two-way voice communication

Clause 6 applies to ICT that supports two-way voice calls — think
business phone systems, video conferencing platforms, or any product
where a real-time voice conversation is a core feature.

## What it covers

- **Real-time text (RTT)** — the ability to send and receive text
  character-by-character as it's typed, alongside or instead of voice,
  so a deaf or hard-of-hearing user (or someone who can't speak) can
  participate in a live call.
- **Interoperability with captioned telephony** — the product shouldn't
  block or degrade the use of captioned-relay services that transcribe
  the other party's speech in real time.
- **Alerting** — incoming call alerts must be perceivable both visually
  and non-visually, not relying on sound alone.
- **Voice quality** — where voice is the medium, audio quality
  requirements exist so speech (and lip-reading cues in video) remain
  intelligible.

## Who this applies to

Products whose core function is two-way voice communication: PBX and
softphone systems, video-conferencing tools, and similar. It generally
doesn't apply to closed, dedicated systems with no voice-communication
element at all.
