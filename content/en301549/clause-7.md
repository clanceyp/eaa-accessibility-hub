# Clause 7 — ICT with video capabilities

Clause 7 applies to ICT that delivers video content — media players,
streaming platforms, video-conferencing tools, digital signage with
video, and similar.

## What it covers

- **Captions** — the product must be able to process and display
  captions for prerecorded and, where feasible, live video.
- **Audio description** — support for an additional narration track
  describing key visual information for people who can't see the
  video.
- **Sign language** — where sign-language interpretation is provided
  alongside video, the player must be able to display it without
  obscuring other essential content.
- **User controls** — captions and audio description must be
  selectable by the user (not forced on or off), and player controls
  themselves (play, pause, volume, caption toggle) must be operable
  and perceivable.
- **Synchronisation** — captions and audio description must stay in
  sync with the video and audio tracks.

## Who this applies to

Any product where video is a delivered content type — this sits
alongside Clause 9 (Web) for browser-based video players, and Clause 11
(Software) for video playback inside native apps.
