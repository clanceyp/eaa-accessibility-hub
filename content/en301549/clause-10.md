# Clause 10 — Non-web documents

Clause 10 extends WCAG-equivalent requirements to electronic documents
that aren't themselves web pages, but are distributed as part of a
product or service — a downloadable PDF report, a Word contract
template, an ePub, a spreadsheet.

## What it covers

- **Structure and tagging** — documents need real structural markup
  (headings, lists, tables) rather than purely visual formatting, so
  assistive technology can navigate and interpret them.
- **Reading order** — content must have a sensible, programmatically
  determinable reading order, especially for multi-column or
  form-heavy layouts.
- **Text alternatives** — images and non-text content need alt text;
  purely decorative elements should be marked as such.
- **Form fields** — interactive form fields (in a fillable PDF, for
  example) need labels and must be operable via keyboard and screen
  reader.
- Applies across common formats: PDF, Word/OpenDocument, ePub,
  spreadsheets, and similar.

## Who this applies to

Any organisation distributing documents as part of a product or
service in scope of the Web Accessibility Directive or the EAA —
including documents linked from an otherwise-compliant website.
