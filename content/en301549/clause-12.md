# Clause 12 — Documentation and support services

Accessibility doesn't stop at the product interface. Clause 12 requires
that the documentation and support services around a product are
themselves accessible.

## What it covers

- **Accessible documentation** — user guides, manuals, and other
  product documentation must be available in accessible formats, not
  just as a scanned or image-only PDF.
- **Accessibility feature disclosure** — documentation should describe
  the product's accessibility and compatibility features, so users
  know what's available to them.
- **Accessible support channels** — help desks, technical support, and
  training shouldn't rely solely on channels that exclude some users
  (for example, phone-only support with no text-based alternative).
- Support staff should be able to communicate about a product's
  accessibility features when asked.

## Who this applies to

Any organisation providing user documentation or a support channel
alongside a product or service in scope of the EAA or the Web
Accessibility Directive.
