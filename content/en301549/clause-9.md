# Clause 9 — Web

Clause 9 is the clause most people mean when they talk about “EN 301 549
for websites.” It incorporates the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
(WCAG) by reference, success criterion by success criterion.

## What it covers

- Meeting Clause 9 means meeting **WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA** in full
  (V4.1.1 moves this to WCAG 2.2 AA — see the caveat below).
- Applies to websites, web applications, and web content delivered as
  part of a product or service — including content produced by
  authoring tools and content management systems.
- Covers the full set of WCAG principles: perceivable, operable,
  understandable, robust — text alternatives, keyboard access, colour
  contrast, predictable navigation, compatibility with assistive
  technology, and more.

## Who this applies to

Public sector websites and intranets/extranets under the Web
Accessibility Directive, and private-sector websites and web apps in
scope of the EAA — e-commerce, banking, transport ticketing, e-books,
and similar services delivered over the web.

For the fuller narrative — what's in scope, how the WCAG principles
translate in practice — see the [Web overview](/en301549/web).

full requirement wording, see **EN 301 549 V4.1.1 (2026-09)** directly:

not yet cited in the EU Official Journal, so **V3.2.1** (WCAG 2.1 AA)
remains the version with current legal effect for EAA presumption of
conformity — see the [timeline](/timeline) for the expected citation
date.
